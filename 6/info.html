Understanding European Soccer

Learn about the leagues, competitions, and basic structure of European club soccer.

Domestic Leagues

Soccer clubs compete in domestic leagues within their own countries. Teams earn points throughout the season based on their results.

A win gives a team three points, a draw gives each team one point, and a loss gives no points. The team with the most points at the end of the season usually becomes the league champion.

Leagues on This Website

  • England: Premier League
  • Spain: La Liga
  • Germany: Bundesliga
  • Italy: Serie A

UEFA Champions League

The UEFA Champions League is a major European club competition. Top clubs from leagues across Europe compete against each other for the championship.

Clubs such as Real Madrid, Barcelona, Bayern Munich, Manchester United, Liverpool, AC Milan, Inter Milan, and Manchester City have competed in the Champions League.

Domestic Cups

Clubs can also compete in knockout cup competitions within their countries. Unlike a league season, teams can be eliminated from a cup after losing a knockout match.

  • England: FA Cup
  • Spain: Copa del Rey
  • Germany: DFB-Pokal
  • Italy: Coppa Italia

Promotion and Relegation

European soccer leagues generally use promotion and relegation. Teams that finish near the bottom of a league can be moved down to a lower division.

Successful teams from the lower division can be promoted to replace them. This system gives clubs opportunities to move between different levels of competition.
