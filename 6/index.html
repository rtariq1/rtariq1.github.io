Explore European Soccer

European soccer has some of the most famous clubs, players, stadiums, and competitions in the world. This website helps you learn about clubs from four major soccer countries.

Use the Explore page to first choose a country. Then choose a club to learn about its history, stadium, top scorer, major trophies, Ballon d'Or history, and fun facts.

Countries Included

The European Soccer Explorer includes clubs from England, Spain, Germany, and Italy. Each country includes four clubs for you to explore.
