European Soccer Club Explorer

CSCI 3054 - Project 2

Topic Description

Soccer is the most popular sports in the world, and Europe is home to many famous soccer clubs. However, someone who is new to European soccer may have trouble understanding which teams belong to different countries and learning basic information about each club. My website will make it easier for users to explore soccer clubs from four major European soccer countries: England, Spain, Germany, and Italy. Also, it will show them the best teams from each country.

There are already websites that provide soccer information, including ESPN and UEFA. These websites contain scores, news, schedules, standings, and information about teams. My website will have a simpler purpose. It will allow users to select a country and then a soccer club to learn important information about that club. This can help someone who is new to European soccer learn about different teams in a simple and organized way.

Soccer is an interesting topic to me because I enjoy the sport and follow soccer teams and players. This website will give me the opportunity to build a web application around a topic that interests me while also helping other people and myself learn more about European soccer.

Application Description

Home Page

The home page will introduce the European Soccer Club Explorer. It will explain what the website does and give users an overview of the countries and soccer clubs that they can explore.

Two-Step Soccer Club Explorer

The main interactive page will use a two-step process. First, the user will choose a country. Second, the user will choose one of four soccer clubs from that country. The website will then display information about the selected club.

  1. England
    • Manchester United
    • Manchester City
    • Liverpool
    • Arsenal
  2. Spain
    • Real Madrid
    • Barcelona
    • Atletico Madrid
    • Athletic Bilbao
  3. Germany
    • Bayern Munich
    • Borussia Dortmund
    • VfB Stuttgart
    • Borussia Monchengladbach
  4. Italy
    • Juventus
    • Inter Milan
    • AC Milan
    • Genoa

Club Information

After the user selects a club, the website will display the same types of information for each of the 16 clubs. The information will include the club name, city, stadium, founding year, club colors, major trophies, a short history, and an image or two about the club.

Additional Information Page

The third page will provide general information about European soccer that is different from the information on the club explorer page. It will explain the major soccer leagues in England, Spain, Germany, and Italy and provide basic information about how European soccer leagues work.

Contact Us Page

The fourth page will be a Contact Us page. The page will eventually allow users to provide their name, email address, favorite soccer club, and a message or question about the website.
